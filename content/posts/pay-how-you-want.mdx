Most booking platforms ask for a card before you can do anything useful.
We think that's the wrong call for a car wash marketplace in South Africa.

## The reality of how South Africans pay

Cash is not a fallback here. For millions of people, cash is the primary payment method — by preference,
by habit, and by the structure of daily life. The same goes for car wash operators.
Many run entirely on cash. Many have a Speedpoint machine. Almost none started with online payments.

Building a marketplace that requires Paystack to function means building for a minority of the market
from day one. We were not interested in doing that.

## Three ways to pay

Vroom Valet supports three payment modes. The operator decides which ones apply to their wash.

**Cash at collection.** The most common mode in South Africa. You book, you get washed, you pay
the operator directly when you collect the car. Vroom Valet never touches the money.

**Card at the till.** The operator's own Speedpoint or card machine. You swipe at the end, the
same way you would at any other business. Again, Vroom Valet is not involved in the transaction.

**Online via Paystack.** Where operators have enabled it, you can pay upfront in the app.
Paystack processes the payment and routes the operator's share directly to their bank account.

## No card required to sign up

You can create a Vroom Valet account, book a wash, and pay in cash without ever entering a card number.
The card field does not exist on the signup screen. It appears only if you choose to pay online.

This is a deliberate choice. The hardest part of building a marketplace is getting the first customer
through the door. Putting a payment form between "download the app" and "book a wash" is a door we
chose not to build.

## How we make money

Our platform revenue comes from two places.

**Operator subscriptions.** Car wash operators pay a monthly fee to list their wash on Vroom Valet.
The Starter tier covers cash and card-at-till operators. The Growth tier adds Paystack integration.

**Transaction take-rate.** On Paystack-routed transactions, we take a small platform fee.
This only applies when the operator has chosen to enable online payment. Cash and card-at-till
operators pay nothing beyond their subscription.

We can only collect a take-rate on transactions we are part of. Cash and card-at-till keep the
operator's full revenue. That is also deliberate — it means we only make more money when online
payment genuinely works for operators, not by forcing it.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us directly

| Information | When we collect it |
|---|---|
| Name, email address, phone number | When you create an account |
| Profile photo | If you choose to upload one |
| Vehicle details (make, colour, registration) | When you add a vehicle to your account |
| Payment card details | Processed and stored by Paystack — we never see or store raw card numbers |

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Device location (GPS) | To show car washes near you when you allow location access |
| Address you type | To find car washes near a location you search manually |
| Push notification token (device ID) | To send booking updates and, if you opt in, promotional messages |
| App usage and booking history | To operate the service and improve it |
| Device type, OS version, app version | For compatibility and error diagnosis |
| IP address | For security and fraud prevention |
| Crash reports and error logs | To identify and fix bugs |

In addition to the above, operators provide:
- Business name, address, and operating hours
- Bank account details (collected and verified by Paystack for payment processing)
- Staff member names and contact details

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- **Operate the service** — process bookings, send booking confirmations, connect you with operators.
- **Payments** — route payment transactions through Paystack where you choose to pay in-app.
- **Loyalty programme** — track stamps and rewards earned at participating operators.
- **Notifications** — send you booking status updates (wash received, wash complete, ready for collection). These are transactional and cannot be turned off without disabling notifications entirely.
- **Marketing** — send you promotions, offers, and app updates *only if you have opted in*. You can withdraw consent at any time in Account → Notifications.
- **Safety and security** — detect fraud, investigate disputes, enforce our Terms of Service.
- **Improving the product** — understand how features are used, diagnose errors, plan improvements.
- **Legal compliance** — meet our obligations under South African law, including tax record-keeping.

**Our lawful bases under POPIA:**
- Processing is necessary to perform the contract with you (bookings, payments, account management).
- Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product improvement), balanced against your rights.
- Your consent, where we rely on it (marketing communications).

## 5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account profile and preferences | While your account is active, plus 30 days after a verified deletion request |
| Booking history | 5 years from the date of the booking (SARS record-keeping requirement) |
| Payment and transaction records | 5 years (tax and financial compliance) |
| Device location | Not stored — used in-session only to find nearby car washes |
| Push notification tokens | While your account is active |
| Marketing consent records | Retained indefinitely as proof of consent |
| Error logs and crash reports | 90 days |
| Staff and operator data | Duration of the operator's active subscription, plus 5 years for financial records |

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised so it can no longer be linked to you.

## 6. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction** — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion** — ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations.
- **Objection** — object to the processing of your information for marketing purposes at any time.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on your consent (e.g. marketing), withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
- **Complain** — lodge a complaint with the **Information Regulator of South Africa**:
  - Website: [www.justice.gov.za/inforeg](https://www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/)
  - Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za

To exercise your rights, contact our Information Officer. We will respond within **30 days**.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest for sensitive data.
- Access controls — staff access is limited to what is necessary for their role.
- Secure credential storage on your device using the device's secure enclave (not plain app storage).
- Regular security reviews.

No system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@vroomvalet.co.za.

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected users as required by POPIA.

## 8. Children

Vroom Valet is a general-audience service and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Push notifications and marketing

**Transactional notifications** (booking received, wash in progress, ready for collection) are sent as part of the service. You can disable these in your device settings, but doing so will mean you miss booking updates.

**Marketing notifications and emails** are only sent if you have opted in. You can opt out at any time:
- In the app: Account → Notifications → Marketing
- By clicking "Unsubscribe" in any marketing email
- By contacting us directly
